## About us

Steffny Couture Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at https://www.steffnycouture.co.uk and the Steffny Couture mobile app. We are a couture studio based at 255 High Street, Hounslow, London TW3 1EA, United Kingdom.

For questions about this policy or your data: hello@steffnycouture.co.uk or +44 7834 877992.

## What information we collect

We collect only what we need to run our business and serve you.

### When you book a fitting or place an order
- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- The details of your booking or order (garment type, date, time, description)
- Photos you choose to upload of your garment

### When you submit a contact form or review
- Your name
- Email address
- The contents of your message or review

### When you browse our website
- We use Vercel Analytics, which collects anonymous usage data (page views, country, device type) without cookies. We do not track you across sites.

### What we don't collect
- Payment card details (we use Stripe for payments; they handle card data; we never see your card number)
- Marketing data from third parties
- Cookies for advertising

## How we use your information

- To respond to your enquiries
- To fulfil bookings, orders, and alterations
- To send booking confirmations and updates
- To improve our service
- To comply with legal obligations (e.g. accounting records)

We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

## Who we share your information with

- **Our staff** at Steffny Couture (Steffi, Rohan, and any team members) — to deliver your booking or order
- **Service providers** that help us run the business:
  - Supabase (database, hosted in Ireland) — stores your booking/order data
  - Vercel (website hosting) — serves you the website
  - SendGrid / Postmark (email delivery) — sends you confirmation emails
  - Stripe (payments, when active) — processes your card payments
- **Legal authorities** if required by law (e.g. court order)

We do not share with anyone else.

## How long we keep your information

- Booking and order records: 6 years (UK accounting requirement)
- Contact form submissions: 2 years
- Reviews: indefinitely (you can request deletion)
- Analytics data: anonymous, 24 months

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Object to processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent

Email hello@steffnycouture.co.uk to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days.

If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the UK ICO at https://ico.org.uk.

## Cookies

We use the minimum necessary:
- **Session cookies** for the shopping cart so it works during your visit (not persistent unless you tick "remember me")
- **Vercel Analytics** uses no cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. We do not need a cookie banner because we don't track you.

If you submit a booking, we may use cookies to remember partially-completed wizard state — this is stored locally on your device and never sent to us.

## Children

Our services are not directed at people under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

## International transfers

Our backend (Supabase) is hosted in Ireland. Some of our service providers (Vercel, Stripe) may transfer data to the US under appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).
